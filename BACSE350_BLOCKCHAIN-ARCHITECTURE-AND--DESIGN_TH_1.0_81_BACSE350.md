|**Course Objectives**|||||||



1. To analyze blockchain system architecture components and justify an architecture selection with stated trade-offs. 

2. To develop the ability to implement smart-contract logic with correct behaviour. 

3. To evaluate real-world blockchain applications with attention to security, privacy, performance and governance. 

## **Course Outcomes** 

1. Determine the suitable blockchain architecture for a specific application. 

2. Apply the concept of Bitcoin and underlying technology of transactions. 

3. Design and implement blockchain solutions using Ethereum or Hyperledger technologies to address real-world problems. 

4. Evaluate security, performance and scalability characteristics of a chosen design. 

5. Analyze workload requirements to select an appropriate on-chain, off-chain, or hybrid storage model. 

## **Module:1 Fundamentals of Blockchain 9 hours** 

Blockchain: Importance and Features - Layers of Blockchain - Types: Public, Private, Consortium - Governance and Technical Challenges - Blockchain Components and Concepts - Block Header and Identifiers - Linking of Blocks - Mining and Consensus Mechanisms: Proof of Work (PoW), Proof of Stake (PoS), Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS), Practical Byzantine Fault Tolerance (PBFT) - Transaction Aggregation into Blocks - Mining the Block - Block Validation and Assembly - Chain Selection.
